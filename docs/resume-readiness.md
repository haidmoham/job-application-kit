# Resume-readiness rubric

The purpose of this step is to decide whether the **current** resume is sufficient evidence for the intended job search. It is not a writing task.

Evaluate five dimensions:

- relevance to target lanes;
- coverage of verified ownership and scope;
- coverage of technical depth;
- coverage of outcomes or operational consequences;
- factual consistency with the onboarding interview.

Do not penalize visual simplicity, lack of buzzwords, or absence of invented metrics. Do flag cases where strong verified experience is reduced to generic task descriptions.

Always state confidence explicitly:

```text
Resume readiness: usable_with_targeted_edits
Confidence: high
Confidence reason: The interview and resume describe the same roles, and three high-value ownership details are verified but absent from the document.
```
